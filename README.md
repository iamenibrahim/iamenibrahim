## 🚀 Featured Work

### 🆘 Last-Mile Assist
AI-powered disaster recovery navigation platform built to help users identify relevant recovery resources, understand alerts, and navigate assistance more quickly.

### 🤖 AI & LLM Research
Research focused on AI-empowered analysis of human interactions with urban infrastructure systems, including LLM-based user profiles and agent-based simulation.

### 🔬 Quantum ML
